Controller guide

Plug in or pair (Bluetooth applet ▸ pairing mode on the pad). Xbox, DualShock 4, DualSense, Switch Pro, Joy-Con and generic USB pads are recognised by the kernel with rumble and, where the hardware has them, gyro/touchpad/light bar. * Steam Input (Steam ▸ Settings ▸ Controller) is the easiest way to remap for Steam games and to use gyro. * AntiMicroX (Welcome ▸ Controllers) maps buttons to keyboard/mouse for any app. * Test a pad: jstest-gtk (menu ▸ Games). * Emulators: RetroArch autodetects; standalone emulators list the pad under Input/Controllers. * Bluetooth Xbox controllers: if the pad won't pair, update its firmware on a console/PC once (old firmware lacks BLE).
